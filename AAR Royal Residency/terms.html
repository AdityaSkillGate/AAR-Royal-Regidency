AAR Royal Residency Guest Guidelines

1. Check-In & Check-Out Timings

  • Check-In Time: 12:00 PM
  • Check-Out Time: 11:00 AM
  • Early arrivals or late departures are accommodated upon prior request subject to suite availability.

2. Mandatory Identification

In accordance with local regulations, all adult guests above 18 years of age must furnish original valid government photo ID proofs (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License) upon check-in.

3. Reservation Cancellation & Refund

  • Cancellations notified at least 24 hours prior to check-in are eligible for a full 100% refund or free date modification.
  • Cancellations made within 24 hours of arrival may be subject to a one-night cancellation retention fee.

4. Guest Code of Conduct

  • To preserve the peaceful environment for pilgrims and families, quiet hours are observed from 10:30 PM onwards.
  • Guests will be held accountable for any accidental or deliberate damage caused to hotel property.
