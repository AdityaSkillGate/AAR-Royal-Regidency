Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about check-in, temple proximity, dining, and policies.

Check-In & Booking

What are the standard Check-In and Check-Out times?

Standard Check-In is at 12:00 PM (Noon) and Check-Out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out is subject to room availability and can be requested in advance via WhatsApp.

Can I book directly through WhatsApp?

Yes! We encourage direct WhatsApp booking at +91 98765 43210 to get zero booking commission, customized check-in hours, and instant confirmation from our reception manager.

Is government ID proof mandatory at check-in?

Yes, all adult guests must present a valid Government photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving License, or Passport) upon check-in as per Tamil Nadu hospitality and police regulations.

Rooms & Amenities

Are all rooms air-conditioned?

Yes, 100% of our rooms across all categories (Standard, Deluxe, and Family Suites) are equipped with modern remote-controlled split air conditioning.

Is there 24/7 hot water and generator power backup?

Yes, our solar and electric water heaters ensure 24-hour hot water in all bathrooms, and an automatic heavy-duty generator provides uninterrupted power backup for lights, AC, and elevators.

Is high-speed Wi-Fi complimentary?

Yes, complimentary high-speed optical fiber Wi-Fi is accessible throughout all guest rooms, hallways, and the reception lobby.

Dining & Restaurant

Are Pure Veg and Non-Veg kitchens separated?

Yes, we strictly maintain separate cooking areas, utensils, and staff workflows for pure vegetarian meals and non-vegetarian dishes to respect the spiritual traditions of temple pilgrims.

What are the restaurant dining hours?

Breakfast: 7:00 AM – 10:30 AM | Lunch: 12:30 PM – 3:30 PM | Dinner: 7:00 PM – 10:30 PM. 24-hour room service is available for hot beverages and quick snacks.

Location & Travel

How far is the residency from Sri Sankaranarayanar Temple?

AAR Royal Residency is located just 300 meters (~5 minutes leisurely walk) from the main gopuram entrance of Sri Sankaranarayanar Temple.

Do you have on-site car and bus parking?

Yes, we offer spacious, secure private parking that accommodates both personal four-wheelers and tour buses under 24/7 CCTV surveillance.

Payment & Cancellation

What payment modes are accepted?

We accept all major UPI platforms (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Debit & Credit Cards, Net Banking, and Cash payments at the reception desk.

What is your cancellation policy?

Direct bookings can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 24 hours before your scheduled arrival date. Contact our front desk directly for quick assistance.
