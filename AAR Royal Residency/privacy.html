Guest Privacy Policy

At AAR Royal Residency, we are deeply committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our guests' personal data.

1. Information We Collect

When booking a room or contacting our front desk, we may collect:

  • Guest name, contact telephone/WhatsApp number, and email address.
  • Government photo identification details as required by local authorities during check-in.
  • Stay dates, room preferences, and special dietary requests.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To confirm room reservations, send booking receipts, and assist with arrival logistics.
  • To provide seamless customer care and respond to inquiries via phone, WhatsApp, or email.
  • To fulfill legal, security, and statutory hotel registry requirements.

3. Data Protection & Non-Disclosure

We do not sell, lease, or rent your personal information to third parties. Data is strictly utilized for the administration of your hospitality services at our property.
